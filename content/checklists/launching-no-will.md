topic: "Beneficiaries and a will"
prompt: "If something happened to you, would anyone actually know your wishes — do you have a beneficiary named anywhere, or a will?"
actions:
  - "Name a beneficiary on every account that allows it — 401(k), IRA, life insurance, bank accounts."
  - "Draft a simple will, even a basic one; it's far better than leaving the decision to state default rules."
  - "Revisit both any time your relationship status changes."
---
Most people this age assume they're too young for this to matter. The point isn't the document, it's that decisions get made by default (state law, whoever's listed on an old form) instead of on purpose.
