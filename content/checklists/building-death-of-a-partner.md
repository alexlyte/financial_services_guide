topic: "If a partner passes away"
prompt: "Have you talked about what happens financially if one of you passes away unexpectedly — not just emotionally, but literally: whose name is on what, and who inherits it?"
actions:
  - "Review how each account is titled and who's named as beneficiary — update anything left over from before the relationship."
  - "Get term life insurance sized to replace the income the household actually depends on."
  - "Write down where key documents and account logins are so the surviving partner isn't searching blind."
---
Couples at this stage have usually discussed the emotional side of loss but rarely the mechanical side: accounts, titling, and who has access to what.
