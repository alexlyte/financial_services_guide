prompt: "Do you have accessible cash outside of investments, so a market downturn doesn't force you to sell at a bad time?"
actions:
  - "Keep a cash reserve outside your investment accounts sized to a year or more of expenses."
  - "Replenish that buffer during up markets instead of waiting until it's empty."
  - "Treat the buffer as insurance against selling low, not as idle money to invest."
---
A cash buffer is what turns a market drop from an emergency into a non-event — it buys time to wait the market out instead of selling into it.
